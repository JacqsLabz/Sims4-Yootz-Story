Sara knocked on the glass double doors. After a moment, Lilith came around the corner on the other side of the large windows in the doors. She opened the door, and stepped back to let Sara through, "Welcome fledgling."

"We need to talk." Sara said to Lilith, skipping any real greeting as she made her way in the door. 

"That is what we have a parlor for, this way please." Lilith lead Sara to the living room. "Caleb, this is Sara, the one I told you about." She said to the man sitting at the chess table, then turned to Sara. "Sara, this is my brother Caleb."

![](../assets/img/02.04/12-12-17_6-50-14%20PM.png)

Caleb turned then stood to greet Sara. "Welcome to our home, fledgling." 

Sara didn't like the sound of 'fledgling' and her heart pounded; out of fear or anger, she wasn't sure which. She swallowed. The though *'he's like her'* echoed in her mind; Sara didn't know how she could tell, or even what the thought meant, just that knowing it made her even less at ease. "Charmed I'm sure." She turned to Lilith, "Can we talk, just you and me?"

"Yes, of course, always. Let's sit, shall we?" Lilith gestured to the couch and arm chairs on the other side of the room, then made her way over to them. 

Caleb gave her a slight smile, Sara could have sworn there was a glimmer from a fang in it, before he turned away from her. 

![](../assets/img/02.04/12-12-17_7-16-22%20PM.png)

As Caleb turned back to his chess table, Sara's stomach threatened to bring up everything in it right then and there. The nausea hit her so much faster than usual, and the pain was as sharp as ever. And she hadn't even had anything solid for dinner.

![](../assets/img/02.04/12-12-17_7-16-38%20PM.png)

It was so usual, but she didn't have time to worry about it now. Sara swallowed, then closed her eyes for a moment as she tried to calm down by focusing on her breath. 

When she opened them, she turned to see Lilith in one of the armchairs. She took another steadying breath, and made her way over to her. 

"So what is this, some sort of twisted vampire-act thing? I got back the memory of you pretending to bite my neck, what is going on here? What else did you do to me?" Since that black smoke, Sara's stomach felt worse. But she had more energy than she had had for quite a while. "Act?" Lilith whispered. Her hands went to her face as realization hit. 

![](../assets/img/02.04/12-12-17_6-54-32%20PM.png)

"Wanted what?" Sara just wanted to know what they did to her, and if it had anything to do with her rioting gut. 

Lilith let her fangs show. "The turning bite. My biting you was no fake or performance; my brother and I, we are both vampires. As you yourself now are." 

Sara couldn't… "How could I want something like that, vampires aren't..." She couldn't bring herself to say it. Sara swallowed, she felt as though she had been punched in the gut. Hard. 

"Something drew your soul to darkness, to the dark power and strength of vampires." Lilith stood, and stepped closer to Sara. "Have you been able to connect with it yet? With your affinity for it, manipulating should come easy to you. Of course we can show you how to bend it to your will if you like."

![](../assets/img/02.04/12-12-17_6-53-52%20PM.png)

Lilith's eyebrow furrowed in concern, "Sara?" 

![](../assets/img/02.04/12-12-17_6-53-20%20PM.png)

Sara's stomach churned along with her conflicting mixture of emotions. She swallowed a burp as one thought stood out above the racing haze of confusion her mind: she was about to lose the fight with her gut. "I'm going to be sick, do you even have a toilet?" she managed to get out. 

"Yes, back through the entry way." Lilith pointed toward the nearby archway. "The door on the other side of the stairs." 

Sara rushed past the stairs, though the one lone door,

As she spit it back into the sink, a certain calm settled over her. If she closed her eyes, she could pretend she was at the gym locker room after a workout. All the pain in her stomach had vanished. Sure, some of her muscles felt a bit tired or worn out and she felt a little thirsty, but all of that was familiar. She just needed a little hydration and time. 

Sara sighed at her own thoughts, and then raised her head to look at herself in the mirror. She didn't know **how** she knew it was all true, but she could feel it in her bones: not only were vampires real, she now was one. She just knew Lilith was being honest with her.

There was only one way to find out what this would mean for her pregnancy. So she made sure her mouth was fully rinsed out, the sink was rinsed clean, and the toilet was flushed before she headed back out to the other room.

Sara clenched her fists again. She had used this new vampire ability without even trying; without even knowing she had it. It was kinda chilling. Lilith got quiet and seemed to be deep in concentrating. Part of Sara thought it was odd having someone she barely knew close like this, but part of her was just fine with it. Lilith didn't feel as much like a stranger as she should have, and Sara realized she knew she could trust Lilith. 

After a few moments, "Oh... I see." Lilith closed her eyes, lowered her hands to her sides, and took a deep breath.

"What is it?" Sara couldn't keep all of the fear out of her voice. 

"The child is healthy." Lilith assured her fledging. "Shall we sit? There is something I need to explain." 

Sara felt the darkness dissipate a bit as Lilith changed back to her normal appearance. She joined Lilith on the couch, not sure if this was a bad sign or a good sign. There was plenty of things that would probably need to be explained to her, so maybe this wasn't anything serious. 

"As I said," Lilith started, "Your daughter is healthy. She's a bit thirsty, but that is not usual for a new vampire. As long as you feed here soon, that is not something to worry or be concerned about." 

"It's a girl?" Sara felt her eyes go a bit big. "And she's a vampire?"

"Yes. I felt some some of the vampiric darkness within her." 

A healthy baby girl, and a vampire like herself. Sara felt a sort of... kinship. Just a little. Not that she didn't love her other two daughters, but her and the new baby had been through this change together. 

"Though, I found why you were sick. It is not normal for a vampire to not be able to keep down regular solid food. We of course don't get an nutrients from what humans eat, so the flavor is a little muted, but it is otherwise pleasant for most vampires. This makes blending in easier." 

Sara had no ideas if being the exception to the rule was a good thing in this case. In many cases it wasn't. But Lilith's tone was steady; Sara didn't feel like she was delivering grim news. 

"Some vampires have what is called a withered stomach. The only one I personally know that has it is rather old, but I suppose that's beside the point. Though it does not only affect the stomach. Your digestive tract can no long handle anything solid." 

Sara swallowed, and looked down at her midsection. So her gut was trashed? But she was still pregnant, and she needed to feed both of them. She was a *vampire* that needed to *feed*. "So, you're saying I need to drink blood?" At first thought made her skin crawl. But as she pictured doing so, a thirsty hunger awoke within her. One that made the idea sound so wonderful... that part of her body shivered. Sara wasn't sure rather it was from disgust, fear, or... desire. 

"According to that vampire, fresh blood is the only thing they can consume. The only thing that does not make them ill." That was all Lilith knew, she had no way to verify such claims. 

It wouldn't be the first time Sara had needed to make major changes to her diet, but it would certainly be.... different. Sara wasn't sure how she felt about it, but two things stood out to her. First, she had to admit: the idea of not painfully puking her guts out sounded wonderful. That alone made her curious. But secondly, and more importantly, the bottom line was that she was pregnant. Regardless of how she felt about it, there was no getting around how much her body, and her growing baby, needed proper nutrition.
