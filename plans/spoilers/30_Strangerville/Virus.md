Don gets infected with the Strangerville virus, so Sara throws everything she has into helping him. 

She also wraps her hands, wrist, and knuckles. Like for boxing, it helps her feel study, stable, and ready to fight (even if she might not need it with the vampirism thing, it's something to help her cope).
