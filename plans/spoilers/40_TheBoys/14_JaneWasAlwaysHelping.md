Jane got sat down and smiled over at Grant, "What do you think Grant dear?" 

Grant took a bite of the popcorn from his bowl, and was caught off guard. It tasted just like when he made it. Which came from a recipe card in Savanna's handwriting he'd found in the apartment. All of it was the same: the butter, the sugar, all the seasonings, everything was just right. The realization hit him all at once: it was never Savanna's recipe, it was Jane's all along. 

He swallowed and smiled up at Jane, "Thank you, it's great." Jane didn't know it, but she had helped them get through some of the hardest times in that apartment, because Grant has used the same popcorn recipe countless times to cheer up Ben. Enough times that Grant didn't even know where the card was anymore, he just knew how to make it off the top of his head. Though if Grant was honest, really it had become a comfort to both of them. Grant didn't even know how to put words to how much it meant to him.
