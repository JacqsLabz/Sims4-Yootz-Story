I don't think he'll use the literal phasing of my body doesn't know, I think the phrasing of just some part of me is more natural. Because once he has the self-awareness to realize that there's a part of him that is still hyper worried about Ben, it doesn't fix everything overnight, but it helps him understand what's going on. It stops being confusing. Because I think on some level he would understand that it makes sense for some part of him to still be scared about Ben's safety and well-being, because well I mean he was so focused on it for so long. He comes to understand it as metaphorically sinking through the bed and the floor, because again he hasn't learned this new state yet. 

I have something I like with these two characters is how much they have a sort of kinship and understanding that is just unspoken. For example: Both of them have realized that they share Jane's rare eyes, neither one of them have talked about it. They don't need to. And even if she's surprised about him making her tea after her bad dream, he could just be like well that's what you made me so I assumed that helps you. If she bothers to say anything, she might not. She might not feel the need to. He does make that assumption, but she might also just figure out that he did without him needing to say anything about it.
