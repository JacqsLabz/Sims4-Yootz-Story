Sara sat with her coffee, and pulled out her phone. She passed the time catching up some sports news and browsing her favorite athlete focused blogs, because she didn't feel any need to watch for the person she was meeting. Anyone who wasn't entirely blind could spot her blood-red hair. Even in a large crowd, and definitely in a coffee shop. 

"Are you Sara?" Came an unfamiliar sweet feminine voice. 

Sara looked up, and damn... she was even cuter in person than in her profile picture. "Yeah, that's me." Sara tried to focus, they were here to talk about potentially being just roommates, nothing more. But dang did that mini skirt, tights, and boots look good on her. 

She sat her adorable self down in the chair opposite Sara. "I'm Jane, obviously I guess." She gripped her cup. There was an awkward pause. 

Why had Sara let her case worker talk her into looking for a roommate in the first place? Right: she couldn't afford her own place. Life would be much easier when she could, but that would take time. 

"Sorry, I guess I'm not great at knowing what to say when I meet people." Jane looked down. 

"Me neither. Most people are..." maybe intimidated wasn't best word, "put off by my tattoos." Especially recently with how her build had started to shift to being more muscular. Though the blood red hair didn't help. 

Jane rolled her eyes, "Tell me about it. Most people are so weird about things that are cool."

"Too true." Sara half smiled. 

"The number times I've been told 'it's not Halloween' - as if I need a holiday to wear my favorite outfits. I don't think most people even notice my tattoo."

Sara tilted her head. "Oh?" And what tattoo was that? She didn't see one, but there was plenty of Jane's skin she couldn't currently see. 

"Well, I mean, I guess it being on my back makes it harder to see." Jane stood and turned.

Sara saw the edge of her feathered wings tattoo sticking out past her black tank top, after she peeled her eyes away from Jane's lovely butt that is. "Cool, do they take up your whole back?"

"Eh, most of it." She turned back around, "Ok weird question:" she sat down, "Who does your hair? The color is just ***so*** vivid." Her eyes lit up in a way that made her even prettier somehow. 

Sara smirked, this woman was too freaking cute, "Actually, this is my natural color." she admitted. 

"Really? You're so lucky! My natural color is so boring." 

"Is is the black?" Sara wondered out loud. Though, she supposed it could all be dyed. 

"Yup, only one of the most common hair colors. It blends in with basically everything. Hence why I add color to it." Jane turned her head to show off her french braid. 

"It looks nice." Sara smiled. 

"Thanks." Jane smiled back. 

----

"Well, I guess we could talk about stuff like deal breakers?" Sara asked 

"Oh, yes. I suppose that's important." Jane looked down at her hands. "Well, the biggest thing for me is I want a space for my easel. And to hang a few painting on the walls." She looked up again, her eye sparkled, "I love painting and art, can't live without it." 

"I'm fine with that." A little decor certainly wouldn't hurt, it wasn't like Sara had any. 

"Awesome." Jane's smile was adorable. "What about you?"

"Well," Sara said, "I have some workout equipment I would like to have space for. It's in storage now, but I have a punch bag and a treadmill, I'd really like a space for at least one of them." 

"So hobby space: plenty of it." Jane nodded, and her whole body bobbing along with the movement. "Anything else that's important to you furniture wise?"
