
Sara sat with her coffee, and read some sports news on her phone. She didn't feel any need to watch for the person she was meeting; anyone could spot Sara's blood-red hair, even in a large crowd, and definitely in a coffee shop. 

"Are you Sara?" Came an unfamiliar sweet feminine voice. 

Sara looked up, and damn... she was even cuter in person than in her profile picture. "Yeah, that's me." Sara tried to focus, they were here to talk about potentially being just roommates, nothing more. 

Jane sat her lovely butt down in the chair opposite Sara. "I'm Jane, obviously I guess." She gripped her cup. There was an awkward pause. 

Why had Sara let her case worker talk her into looking for a roommate in the first place? Right: she couldn't afford her own place. Life would be much easier when she could, but that would take time. 

"Sorry, I guess I'm not great at knowing what to say when I meet people." Jane looked down. 

"Me neither. Most people are..." maybe intimidated wasn't best word, "put off by my tattoos." Especially recently with how her build had started to shift to being more muscular. Though the blood red hair didn't help. 

"Ehk," Jane groaned, "Most people are so weird about things that are cool."

"Too true." Sara half smiled.
