As Jane and Sara are dating, at some point things are getting serious, and so Sara knows she needs to talk to Jane about something rather important. This might be before they become sexually active together, or at least shortly after they do. Definitely before Sara proposes and they get married (and they get married before Sara does eventually get pregnant so this conversation happens a long while before that). 

They were on the couch, Sara holding Jane. Jane looked into her eyes, "We could move to the bed? If you're ready for that?" 

Oh how Sara wanted that. But... there hadn't been a good time to tell Jane yet. 

When Sara hesitated, Jane touched her, "No pressure, we can wait if you want to. Promise I don't mind."

"It's not that I don't want to." Sara kissed her, "I've been fantasizing about that with you... for a while now." Sara's voice was husky and rough, so she tried to take a deep breath and get it back to normal, "But if we're going to do that, there's some things you should know first." 

Jane was both excited and hesitant. Sara has fantasized about her? About them? Oh, how good that felt to know. And yet clearly this mattered to Sara. "Things like what?" There were too many possibilities that entered Jane's mind to make any type of guess. 

Sara swallowed. "Years ago," This was hard to talk about, "back when I was still a teen," but Jane deserved to know, "it's a long story, but the bottom line is," she wanted Jane to know, "a doctor examined me and told me I'm likely infertile. That I'd probably never get pregnant or have kids of my own." Sara found that it helped to look at this as protecting Jane from not knowing. "And that was before my training got... serious. The intense workouts come with side effects. One that's really common is that my cycles are irregular. Which makes pregnancy unlikely even for people that don't have other fertility issues. I just..." Sara paused, "I don't know if kids are a deal breaker for you." It hurt to say, but it was better to rip off the band-aid now before they got physically involved. Before things got even more serious between them, because then it would hurt even more. True, Jane hadn't really talked about babies, but Sara couldn't have them, and now Jane knew that. Sara tried to just wait as Jane thought. It was hard to wait, but it helped reminding herself that she had had years to think about this, and it made sense that Jane might need a few moments. It wasn't a small thing. 

Jane was quiet as she turned it over in her mind. There was not only one, but two different things that made pregnancy unlikely for Sara. The things was, did that bother Jane? Were children a deal breaker for her? She loved Sara, she knew that. But how did she feel about being with Sara knowing they might not have a family? But wait, that wasn't the only way. "Well, what do you think about adopting?" Jane realized that she did want a family - with Sara. One way or another. As long as Sara was ok with it. 

Sara hadn't considered that. Though she hadn't expected to find a lover at all. She really wished they could have children of their own, but given that they couldn't, she supposed at least then that way they would have family. So she nodded. "Yeah, we could look into that. Some day."

Jane felt warm relief at being on the same page about this. "Yeah, later on. When we have a bigger house."

"And more money." Sara said. Her paychecks right now weren't much, not yet anyway. But in time, they would get established, build careers, and then maybe a family. Not biological children, but Jane was right, they could at least look into adoption. 

"When we're ready, we can figure it out." Jane leaned in for a kiss, and found Sara met her lips with deep passion. They would have a family one way or another when they were ready. In the mean time, Jane couldn't keep her hand off Sara. When she came up for air from all the kissing, "Now, about those fantasies," she looked deep into Sara's eyes.
