
Sara sighed, this was the fourth house they were going to look at, and hopefully it was better than the others. It was a one bedroom, but adding on to it would be an option later. 

"Oh, now that's cute!" Jane spun to face Sara, a happy twinkle in her eye, "And look at that huge side yard; plenty of space to expand." 

Sara couldn't stop her grin at Jane's excitement, she was too cute, "Sure, it's cute on the outside, but let's look at the inside." Sara when up the stairs to the porch. This one had a distinct downside: it was a one bedroom. Though that side yard was definitely big enough to add another bedroom onto the house, if they ended up living together long enough to both save up enough for that. Either way, Sara wanted to save up money. Either for that or getting her own room or her own place. 

"I like this porch," Jane's eyes lit up as she looked around on the porch, "Oh! I could put my easel out here. Then I'd have so much natural light." She gestured to the half of the porch on the other side of the front door. 

"Yeah, that would work." Sara agreed, it would certain be out of the way. 

They went through the front door, and Sara found herself nodding again. "Now this is nice. Plenty of space to put my punch bag over in that corner."

"Definitely." Jane nodded, "Treadmill too, I'd guess?" 

"Maybe? That would take up a bit more space." Sara pointed out. 

Jane shrugged, "We can at least try it." She turned, "That table is cute, and that's the kitchen?" She came around the wall. "Galley style, so lots of counter space. For my tea maker." The adorable excited look was back.

"Good stuff." Sara went past the kitchen, it wasn't that big, but it certainly would do. She opened the door to the bedroom. It wasn't as big as she had hoped, but it would certainly do. The main thing that jumped out her was the bed. A single double bed. 

"Oh." Jane paused, "Well, Um, I guess we'll need another bed? Oh, I don't really have money for one. Not after what it'll cost to move in here... well, if we go with this place."

Sara didn't have enough for a bed either, but she shrugged and put her hands on her pockets. "I can get a cot for pretty cheap." She'd slept on far worse before. 

"Are you sure?" Jane turned to her. 

"Yeah, it'll just be temporary anyway. I'll be fine." Though with how cute Jane was- No, they barely knew each other, there was no need to think about that. The cot would do. 

"Well if you're sure, then I suppose that would work. I guess we could push the bed against the wall?" Jane pointed to one side of the room. 

Sara smirked. "Yeah, that sounds good." It didn't look too heavy, Sara would bet she could move it without issue.
