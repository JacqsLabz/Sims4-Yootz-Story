Sara sighed, this was the fourth house they were going to look at. The others they had viewed just hadn't fit what they were looking for. This one was a one bedroom, but they would have the option of adding on to it later (to say, make it a two bedroom). That option was what made this house worth looking at. 

"Oh, now that's cute." Jane spun to face Sara, a happy twinkle in her eye, "And look at that huge side yard; plenty of space to expand." 

Sara couldn't stop her grin at Jane's excitement, she was too cute, "Sure, it's nice on the outside, but let's look at the inside." Sara went up the stairs to the porch. 

"I like this porch," Jane's eyes lit up as she looked around on the porch, "Oh! I could put my easel out here. Then I'd have so much natural light." She gestured to the half of the porch on the other side of the front door. 

"Yeah, that would work." Sara agreed, it would certain be out of her way, so it wouldn't matter to her. Sara wasn't sure Jane would feel the same about the porch come winter, but maybe they would have a backup spot chosen by then. 

Sara pulled out her phone for the temporary code, typed it into the lockbox, and then got the front door open. They each made their way inside, and Sara found herself nodding again. "Now this is nice. Plenty of space to put my punch bag over in that corner." 

"Definitely." Jane nodded, "Treadmill too, I'd guess?" 

"Maybe? That would take up a bit more space." Sara pointed out. She didn't want Jane to feel like she'd taken over the living room, the plan was split whatever place they found 50/50.

Jane shrugged, "We can at least try it." She turned, "That table is cute, and that's the kitchen?" She came around the wall. "Galley style, so lots of counter space. So plenty of space for my tea maker." The adorable excited look was back. 

"Good stuff." Sara went past the kitchen, it wasn't that big, but Jane was right about the countertops. For as small as it was, it help plenty of space for appliances. She opened the door to the bedroom. It wasn't as big as she had hoped. But more importantly: There was one bed. A single double bed. 

"Oh." Jane paused, "Well, Um, I guess we'll need another bed? Oh, I don't really have money for one. Not after what it'll cost to move in here... well, if we go with this place."

Sara didn't have enough for a bed either, but she shrugged and put her hands on her pockets. "I can get a cot for pretty cheap." She'd slept on far worse before. 

"Are you sure?" Jane looked at her. 

"Yeah, it'll just be temporary anyway. I'll be fine." Though with how cute Jane was- No, they barely knew each other, there was no need to think about that. The cot would do until she saved up enough for her own bed. 

"Well if you're sure, then I suppose that would work. I guess we could push the bed against the wall? To make space?" Jane pointed to one side of the room. 

"Yeah, that sounds good." It didn't look too heavy, Sara bet she could move it without issue.
