Shortly after the camping trip, one day Savanna just screamed. 

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_10-50-43%20PM.png)

"I can't take this house anymore!" Savanna yelled. 

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_10-52-05%20PM.png)

"What do you mean sweetie?" Sara put on an uneasy smile.

"Maybe we should all go sit on the couch." Jane said.

They did, and Savanna glared at them, "You make sure nothing in this house is ever normal. Vampires, ghosts, and you make me pretend like everything is fine!"

"Savanna," Jane started, "that's not fair to us." Jane didn't know where to start; they couldn't help that Emma has been a ghost, and they didn't make her pretend anything. 

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_10-54-28%20PM.png)

Sara knew that wasn't going to help, so she leaned in towards Jane and whispered, "She can't be serious, she's just angry, let me handle this." 

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_10-54-41%20PM.png)

"Of course I'm serious!" Savanna yelled. 

Sara whipped back around to her daughter. "And where will you go? You haven't finished high school yet. What will you do for money?" She snapped in fear, then took a deep breath and put her hands on her thighs. She had to calm herself down, now she wasn't helping things. 

"Exactly." Jane said. "Stay here a few more years, we'll save up some money and help you find something you love to do."

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_11-00-06%20PM.png)

Savanna's voice was flat. "Neither of your are freaking listening to me."

Sara stopped. How many times had she said those exact words, when actually she had been the teen that was refusing to listen to anyone around herself? Sara took a deep grounding breath, "Honey, we just think-" Sara started. 

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_10-55-34%20PM.png)

"I don't care what you think! I'm leaving and you can't stop me!" Savanna yelled. "I'm done with both of you monsters!"

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_11-01-30%20PM.png)

Jane's face filled with rage as she stood. "That is no way-" her voice was low.

"Jane, stop. Please." Sara recognized the look on Jane's face from the mirror. "Trust me, before you say something you'll regret." 

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_11-02-18%20PM.png)

Jane let Savanna walk past.

"I know it's hard," Sara said, "but breathe dear. Just like you taught me."

Jane did, and after a few moments collapse on the couch, deflated. "We're losing our other little girl."

![](../assets/img/02.11/12-23-17_11-09-08%20PM.png)

"I know it hurts." Sara reached for Jane's hand. "But I know what I was like at that age." Sara paused. "Tying to stop her will only push her away. She's angry now. If we give her space and enough time, she'll cool down. When she does, she'll come back."

They agreed to start setting aside money for Savanna, and that they would tell her about it when she came back. To show her that they were serious about helping her.

Maybe in a few days (a few hours? A day?), Sara tries sending a text saying she's sorry for snapping and asks if they can try to talk again. Trying to be the "bigger person" because she is the adult in the situation. 

Sara replies saying the snapping on the couch had nothing to do with her leaving. I was the years of being the only human in the house. 

Or maybe I can work that in above? 

"I'm sick of being the only normal human!" Savanna yelled. Her tone went rude, mocking her sisters, *"What does blood taste like? Ug, needing to use doors is so annoying."*

Sara clinch a fist to ground herself, "What does it matter if your sisters are different from you?" 

"What does it matter?" Savanna repeated as if Sara was out of her mind. "What does it matter if I'm surrounded by monsters?"

Sara opened her mouth to speak, but Savanna cut her off before she even made a sound. 

"I saw you on the camping trip, so don't even. All vampires are like that, aren't they? Monsters with fangs, glowing eyes, and messing with people's minds to drink their blood. It's not only you is it?" Savanna said to Sara, then turned to Jane, "How often do you go out and hunt people? What about Lilith and Don? Does Anne? Is that why she was asking?" 

Jane gasped and put a hand over her mouth. She didn't know how Savanna could say things like calling them monsters. She blinked away tears; didn't their own daughter know what they were like? 

Sara decided to ignore the obviously mean things said in anger, and instead went for what was directly untrue. "No, Anne doesn't have fangs. She is... different from everyone else." Somehow she managed to keep her voice calm despite the feelings in her. She would deal with those later. 

"Everyone in this house is different. I don't know what you think that thing you call Emma is, but she is not your daughter." 

"Do not talk about your sister that way." Jane's tone was brittle and full of ice. It was one thing to call a pair of vampires monsters, but this was something else. 

"Look, we're all upset here. Let's take some time to cool down and-"

"No, I'm done with this house! I'm leaving!" Savanna insisted. 

"You can't just leave. Where will you go?" Sara snapped. 

"Are you going to stop me!? Take control of my mind!?" Savanna screamed.

Jane again found herself so hurt she was at a loss for words for a moment. 

"No." Sara said simply, leaning forward on the couch and rubbed her face. 

"Then I'm out of here." Savanna stormed off to her room to pack a bag. 

"But..." Jane didn't even know how to put it into words. 

"We all need to calm down. This will give her some time to cool down." Sara said.
