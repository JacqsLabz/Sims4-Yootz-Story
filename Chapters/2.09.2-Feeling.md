
## Anne feels it too

Sara had the night off work, and was sitting at the bar in the kitchen late in the night. It was her first night off since she'd gone back to work and, in a way, didn't know what to do with herself. Jane was spending some time painting, but with the girls asleep Sara didn't want to do one of her noisy workouts. 

Sara heard one of the bedroom doors, then Anne came out from the hallway. 

"What are you doing up sweetie?" Sara asked. As she was growing up, how much sleep Anne needed had been getting more and more inconsistent for the past few years. Like as if her body couldn't decide if it needed as much sleep as a human or as little as a vampire. Anne didn't seem in any way phased by it; she had energy like a normal kid and it didn't seem to bother her.
