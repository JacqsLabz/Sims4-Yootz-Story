## Seeing if Emma is there

But Anne's comments (about Emma being there) force Sara to choose between: Anne is lying / in denial / making this all up, or Sara's vampire senses are not misleading her (and are not part of Sara's own denial). 

So Sara starts trying things to try to better connect, to understand what she feels, to see if Emma is there, and maybe eventually to better communicate with Emma. 

Maybe this is to some degree, because Sara fear to hope. Because even if Caleb confirms that a spirit can linger, Sara might want to be more sure on rather it is actually Emma's spirit that she feels. 

Meanwhile Jane isn't sure about it and Savanna hates all the "weird stuff" and just wants a normal childhood where she doesn't come home to her mother trying to do a séance in the living room. 

Which puts Sara in this place where she knows what it sounds like and she knows what it looks like, but because of what it feels like she can't give up this quest. Because if there's even a small chance she is feeling Emma, then not pursuing this would be abandoning her daughter when she needs Sara the most. I mean Sara isn't going to be reckless about this, and like bankrupt the family or anything that, she still fully wants to protect the living members of her family, but she can't give up on this and just ignore her vampire senses telling her Emma is there and sometimes that Emma is sad or happy or whatever Emma sends her.
