Emma made herself a bloody mary, then joined the others at the table as Sara was telling a story. 

![[../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-22-16 AM.png]]

When Sara finished, Jane smiled at Emma, "I made plenty of salad and put it in the fridge. Feel free to grab yourself a bowl or two sweetie."

"I think just stick to this for now," Emma held her cup, "But thanks mom."

"She hasn't been feeling well lately." Anne started. 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-20-31%20AM.png)

Sara shifted in her chair, "What's wrong dear?" 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-23-06%20AM.png)

Jane got up to clean the dishes and Emma held her belly, "I'm not sure; here recently I've felt so strange, especially my stomach. I don't know, I guess my gut has been hurting and upset off and on since the aliens."

Sara listened intently. "How about we see what I can feel with my power, ok?" 

Emma nodded, "Yes, I'd like that." It would be nice to have a better understand of how her sort-of-ghost, sort-of-vampire weirdness was interacting with whatever the aliens had done. It would be nice to know what was going on. And her mom could always feel these things. 

So they stood at the end of the table. 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-32-27%20AM.png)

Sara took a moment to really focus on her dark magic, then she felt another magic that she hadn't felt in a while: alien magic. But it was concentrated in... Emma's womb? She shifted her magic to get a better feel, and couldn't believe what she felt: it was small, but it was fully alien, just like the ones that had abducted Sara for a brief time. "Emma...." Sara tried to start, but she lost the words before she could say them. 

Emma waited at first, but then Sara didn't say more. Why wasn't she saying more? "What is it Mom? How bad is it?" Emma felt her hands tighten at the fear in her. What had her mother scared into speechlessness? Was she going to become a ghost again? 

"No, it's not bad." It just wasn't supposed to be possible. Sara shook herself. No, clearly it was because she felt it. Sara took a deep breath to center herself before trying again. "Honey, you're pregnant." 

Emma must I have heard wrong. "But I can't be..." She couldn't say the word suddenly. "I mean I haven't been with anyone, not like that." 

"The child is alien." Sara swallowed at her own disbelief, they had said vampires weren't compatible hosts. 

Emma froze and her eyes went big. "What?" She took a step back, on wobbly shaky legs. 

That helped Sara focus. "Let's get you sitting down again, ok?" she helped guide Emma to her chair again, then sat in her own chair. 

Anne's face was covered in surprise, "So we're pregnant at the same time? Emma is going to have a child too? What are the odds of that?"

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-26-17%20AM.png)

The word 'child' echoed in Emma's head, until it turned into an otherworldly tone. "So that's what the grim reaper meant..." Emma put her hand to forehead. "Oh gosh." 

Emma wasn't sure if Sara or Anne said anything else, everything became a haze as she felt lightheaded and even more out of sorts than before. 

"I think I need to lay down." Emma said. She tried to stand but found herself even more unsteady on her feet. 

Sara was around the table in an instant. "Careful sweetie." Sara held her up. "Let me help you, ok?"

Emma bit her lip and nodded, "Ok Mom." Her voice was soft and sad. 

Sara picked up Emma, "Do you want a bed or your coffin downstairs?"

"Maybe just the couch..." Emma whispered, she suddenly didn't want to be alone. 

Sara nodded then carried Emma over to the long couch near the fireplace. Sara sat on one end of it, and put Emma's head in her lap. She stroked her daughter's shoulder. Sara knew a thing or two about pregnancy being unexpected, what had always helped her had been Jane's touch. The contact, and the connection. She didn't know how this was happening to her daughter, but really the how didn't matter. It was. And all she could do now was help her deal with it. 

In the kitchen, Jane just stood there. Not sure what to do. Sara always knew what to do. 

Just then, Cass started to cry. Jane turned to the door, but she heard Anne. "I'll get her. She's probably hungry." 

Jane turned and watched Anne get up, come into the kitchen, grab Cass's favorite snack from the fridge, and go into the bedroom in the front of the cabin where Cass had been sleeping. 

A mother comforting and feeding a daughter. Surely there was something Jane could do? She turned back to the living room, but this time her eyes wondered to  the TV above the fireplace. Emma always loved movie night popcorn. 

Jane wasn't sure if it would help, but she started looking through the cabinets. She was pretty sure- yes there is was. Jane put the popcorn machine on the counter. Next she put on some water to boil for tea, and let it heat up while she started looking for the ingredients for the popcorn.

So it's easy for Sara to reach it. 

"I guess popcorn sounds nice." Emma whispered. 

Jane handed the bowl to Sara then got herself a cup of tea and set on the armchair facing the couch. 

They just sat like that for a little while, Emma nibbling on the popcorn Sara fed her, while Jane sipped on some tea. Part of Sara wished Jane had brought over some bloodfruit. She could sense Emma had been unsteady partly because she was weak; she needed to drink to keep her strength up. 

"That's the last of this bowl." Sara said as she gave Emma the last piece of the popcorn. "Want me to get you some bloodfruit or something dear?" 

Emma sighed, "I was tying your drink because my stomach's been too upside of solid bloodfruit here lately. I don't know, I'd... kinda like some more popcorn?" She turned to Jane. "If that's ok?"

"I made a whole batch of popcorn, I'll be right back with another bowl." Jane stood and gave a little smile. 

"Thank you. It's just... really comforting right now."
