Savanna was trying to get Anne to play, but Anne just stood there. "Girls, it's time to get dressed and go home." 

"But..." Savanna started, then went quiet as she turned to see them.

Anne came up to Sara and put her arms around her legs. Lilith wondered how much the young vampire could sense. Anne still lacked fangs and a thirst, but the child was vampire none the less, Lilith's connection to the darkness did not lie. 

"Anne, take Sara to the locker room, make sure she gets changed into her clothes. Savanna, you're with me." How would Lilith explain this to Emma's twin sister? 

"I want to go with mom." Savanna took a step back. 

"Then let's go get dressed." Lilith sighed. Even the human child could tell something was wrong. Though Sara was not the type of person to shut down like this, so perhaps it was obvious.

"Doesn't Emma have to get dressed too?" Savanna asked. 

"Emma... has passed away." Lilith turned the urn to show the name on it.

"You're lying." The child ran for the locker room. 

Lilith didn't want to user her vampiric speed in public, so she merely followed the child at a slow human speed. She needed to get dressed too.

"Girls." Lilith made her voice more firm.

"Girls!" Lilith raised her own voice. 

They both turned to her. 

"I'll take care of Sara, both of you put on your regular clothes. Now. We are going home." Lilith made her voice serious, and step between them.

Savanna scowled, but did as she was told. Anne held out the jacket. "Sorry Lilith." 

"It's ok, just please get dressed so we can leave." Lilith accepted the jacket, and then set the urn on the top shelf inside a locker to keep it safe then put the towel over Sara's hair. 

When Lilith went to rub the towel around to dry it, Sara's hand stopped her. "I got it." Her voice was raspy. "Get yourself dressed." 

Once Savanna was dressed, she sat on a bench, legs and armed crossed. Anne ended up helping Sara a bit, and Lilith simply pulled her clothes on over her dry swim suit. She hadn't even gotten in the water with how bright the sun was.

"Again I'm so sorry." Lilith said and wiped away the fresh tears.

Jane didn't seem to hear her. She didn't respond.

"Are we going to have lunch? I'm hungry." 

"Yeah, um," Right, Savanna and Anne both needed regular food. "Do you want to sit with Jane and I'll figure out lunch?" Lilith wasn't sure how close the two were, but maybe Anne would cling to Jane like she had Sara. 

"I guess." Anne came near, and waited.

As Lilith made her way to the kitchen, she heard faint sobbing, definitely from Sara's workout room this time. It gave Lilith pause. The young vampire struggled with her anger sometimes, but she often preserved through whatever lay before her. Had this broken her strong spirit?

Standing in the kitchen, Lilith shook herself. The girls needed food. She squeezed her eyes shut and tried to remember anything about using a modern kitchen... but nothing came to her. Nothing, except that they were full of fire hazards. She didn't want anything else to go wrong today, so she was not going to simply randomly press buttons when she had no skill at cooking. But then Lilith realized: Don was newly turned. Surely he would know how this blasted contraption worked. Not to mention, maybe he would be able to help Sara in a way Lilith herself could not. From what Lilith could tell, they had been close even before her fledging had given him the bite. She called him, and explained that she was at Sara's place, then asked if he could help with preparing food for the two children.

"Let get this straight," Don said, "You know how to use a smart phone, but not a stove? And you want me to leave work for that?" 

Lilith rolled her eyes, "Why would I brother to learn how to use a modern stove? This device is actually useful to me." She sighed, her tone going flat and dull, "There's been... an accident." She swallowed, not sure she could repeat the words again. She lowered her voice, "I think, I think Sara needs you. I- I am trying to help, but I don't know how to help her deal with this other than feeding her daughters, but I have not cared for humans in decades." Lilith sniffed. "So yes, I am requesting your assistance with a stove."

Don clicked his tongue, "I'll be there as soon as I can." And he hung up.
