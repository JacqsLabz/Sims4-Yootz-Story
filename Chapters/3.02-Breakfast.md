![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-22-30%20AM.png)

Emma made herself a bloody mary, then joined the others at the table as Sara was telling a story. 

![[../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-22-16 AM.png]]

When Sara finished, Jane smiled at Emma, "I made plenty of salad and put it in the fridge. Feel free to grab yourself a bowl or two sweetie."

"I think just stick to this for now," Emma held her cup, "But thanks mom."

"Emma, don't just brush it off like that." Anne told her sister, "You agreed it was time to tell them." 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-20-31%20AM.png)

When Emma didn't speak up, Sara shifted in her chair, "What's going on dear?" 

"I guess..." Emma tried, how did she even put it into words? "I've been feeling off for a little while." She drank the last of her bloody mary. 

"It started with the aliens." Anne reminded her. 

"Yes, even since I got abducted that one night,"

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-23-06%20AM.png)

Jane got up to clean the dishes and Emma held her belly, "I've felt so strange, especially my stomach. I don't know, I guess my gut has had these waves of pain. I don't know what's causing it. But the bloodfruit salads have been making my stomach upset more recently." Not to mention she didn't have much energy and felt so bloated, but she wasn't sure she wanted to talk about that part. 

Sara listened intently. "How about we see what I can sense, ok?" 

Emma nodded, "Yes, I'd like that." It would be nice to have a better understand of how her sort-of-ghost, sort-of-vampire weirdness was interacting with whatever the aliens had done. It would be nice to know what was going on. And her mom could always feel these things. 

So they stood at the end of the table. 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-32-27%20AM.png)

<!-- Sara didn't even need to enter her dark form to feel it. -->

Sara took a moment to really focus on her dark magic, then she felt another magic that she hadn't felt in a while: alien magic. But it was concentrated in... Emma's womb? She shifted her magic to get a better feel, and couldn't believe what she felt: it was small, but it was fully alien, just like the ones that had abducted Sara for a brief time. "Emma...." Sara tried to start, but she lost the words before she could say them. 

Emma waited at first, but then Sara didn't say more. Why wasn't she saying more? "What is it Mom? How bad is it?" Emma felt her hands tighten at the fear in her. What had her mother scared into speechlessness? Was she going to become a ghost again? 

"No, it's not bad." It just wasn't supposed to be possible. Sara shook herself. No, clearly it was because she felt it. Sara took a deep breath to center herself before trying again. "Honey, you're pregnant." 

Emma must I have heard wrong. "But I can't be..." She couldn't say the word suddenly. "I mean I haven't been with anyone, not like that." 

"The child is alien." Sara swallowed at her own disbelief, they had said vampires weren't compatible hosts. 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-35-00%20AM.png)

Emma froze and her eyes went big. "What?" She took a step back, on wobbly shaky legs. The world 'child' echoed in her head. 

That pulled Sara out of her thoughts, and helped her focus on the here and now. "Let's get you sitting down again, ok?" she helped guide Emma to her chair again, then sat in her own chair. 

Anne's face was covered in surprise, "I cannot believe we are both with child at the same time. What are the odds of that?"

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-26-17%20AM.png)

The word 'child' kept repeating in Emma's head, until it turned into an otherworldly tone. "So that's what the grim reaper meant..." Emma put her hand to forehead. "Oh gosh." 

Emma wasn't sure if Sara or Anne said anything else, everything became a haze as she felt lightheaded and even more out of sorts than before. 

"I think I need to lay down." Emma said. She tried to stand but found herself even more unsteady on her feet. 

Sara was around the table in an instant. "Careful sweetie." Sara held her up. "Let me help you, ok?"

Emma bit her lip and nodded, "Ok Mom." Her voice was soft and sad. 

Sara picked up Emma, "Do you want a bed upstairs, or your coffin downstairs?"

"Maybe just the couch..." Emma whispered, she suddenly didn't want to be alone. 

Sara nodded then carried Emma over to the long couch near the fireplace. Sara sat on one end of it, and put Emma's head in her lap. She stroked her daughter's shoulder. Sara knew a thing or two about pregnancy being unexpected, what had always helped her had been Jane's touch. The contact, and the connection. She didn't know **how** this was happening to her daughter, but really... the how didn't matter. It was happening. And all she could do now was help her deal with it. 

In the kitchen, Jane just stood there. Not sure what to do. Sara always knew what to do. 

Just then, Cass started to cry. Jane turned to the door, but she heard Anne. "I'll get her. She's probably hungry." 

Jane turned and watched Anne get up, come into the kitchen, grab Cass's favorite snack from the fridge, and go into the bedroom in the front of the cabin where Cass had been sleeping. 

A mother comforting and feeding a daughter. Surely there was something Jane could do? She turned back to the living room, but this time her eyes landed on the TV above the fireplace. Emma always loved movie night popcorn. 

Jane wasn't sure if it would help, but she started looking through the cabinets. She was pretty sure- yes there is was. Jane put the popcorn machine on the counter. Next she put on some water to boil for tea, and let it heat up while she started looking for the ingredients for the popcorn. 

----

After a while, Jane came over to the couch with a bowl of popcorn and a mug of warm chamomile tea. 

<!-- or maybe like a tray that has a tea pot and multiple cups for tea? Jane might have a little herself, and she sits near the couch.  -->

"Hey sweetie." Jane said as she came near the couch. "I made some popcorn and tea, do you want any?" Jane set down the tray on the coffee table. - or maybe like Jane sets them both down on the coffee table, since that's easy to screen shot and makes it kinda like "no pressure" if you will. - or maybe put the bowl like on the arm of the couch or something like that? So it's easy for Sara to reach it. 

"I guess popcorn sounds nice." Emma whispered. 

Jane handed the bowl to Sara, then got herself a cup of tea and sat on the armchair facing the couch. 

They just sat like that for a little while, Emma nibbling on the popcorn Sara handed her, while Jane sipped on some tea. Part of Sara wished Jane had brought over some bloodfruit. She could sense Emma had been unsteady partly because she was weak; she needed to drink to keep her strength up. 

"That's the last of this bowl." Sara said as she gave Emma the last piece of the popcorn. "Want me to get you some bloodfruit or something dear?" Sara knew Emma had never been one for actual blood, but everything was already unusual today. If Emma wanted the real stuff, Sara would get it. 

Emma sighed, "I was tying your drink because my stomach's been too upset for solid bloodfruit here lately. I don't know, I'd... kinda like some more popcorn?" She turned to Jane. "If that's ok?"

"I made a whole batch, I'll grab with another bowl." Jane stood and gave a little smile. 

"Thank you. It's just... really comforting right now." Emma's voice got really soft. "It's familiar, Jane used to make it all the time when I was little."

Sara stroked Emma's hair, "Then I'm glad it helps." Even if it didn't given Emma's vampire body any of what it needed, Sara could feel some of Emma's strength coming back. Sara assumed because it was helping her deal with the emotional shock. Sara sat there, holding her daughter, at the moment just protecting her by being steady. She wished she could do more, but Sara knew all too well, sometimes just being a rock in the storm was already a lot.

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-43-52%20AM.png)

"But want to play with Auntie Emma." Cassandra frowned. 

"I know baby, but she's not feeling well right now." Anne sighed. "Maybe another time sweetie."

----

After a few bowls of popcorn, Sara helped Emma sit up so she could have some of the tea. Sara grabbed a cup for each of them. Emma took a drink, then she looked down at herself, "I guess this explains why I've felt... so tight inside." 

"I suppose that's one way of putting it. For me, it was stretching and fullness." Sara would never forget what it had been like to carry twins. She sipped on her own mug of tea. She wasn't thirsty, but figured it might help Emma feel less alone if they both had some. 

Emma nodded, "I've had a little of that I guess." She took a drink of her tea. 

Sara nodded; that made sense, given that Emma was a vampire instead of human. "Well, is this something you want? Being pregnant that is. You don't have to decide right away, if you want to think about it." 

Emma looked down at her midsection again. "I want a child of my own. I just had decided to wait until Cass was older."

"Life has a habit of... preferring to be unpredictable at times." So much of Sara's own life had been unexpected, from her daughters to her vampirism to her wife Jane. She hadn't expected any of it, and now she couldn't imagine her life without all of them. 

"Yes it does. I mean, I assumed I'd find somebody, and that they would be there to help me through this. But well it's not like I'm alone now." Emma paused. "You're probably right Mom, that some time to think is probably a good idea, at least to sleep on it, but I think I probably want this."

Sara nodded, "Take as much time as you want sweetie. You know we're here for you, no matter what you choose." 

"I know, thank you." Emma smiled, then caught a whiff of what Jane was cooking. She sniffed, trying to take in more of the scent, "Oh that smells so good-" Emma stopped as her stomach growled loudly. She held a hand over her painfully empty belly, and briefly fantasized about biting and chewing what Jane was cooking. Was she *hungry* for *food*? "That's... weird." 

Sara shrugged, "Pregnant bodies do all sorts of weird things, including make odd noises." Especially during Sara's first pregnancy, nothing had felt normal pretty much the whole time. 

Emma wasn't sure how to explain the sensation. Not mention she was a vampire, she couldn't actually need regular human food. But she'd felt better since she had the popcorn. Would she feel even better if she had more food? Well, there was one way to find out. "I think I'm craving some of what Jane is cooking." She turned to Sara, "Do you know if there's any popcorn left?"

"I'll find out dear." Sara set down her cup of tea, then stood and headed to the kitchen. A pregnant vampire craving human food for comfort. It certainly wasn't the weirdest thing Sara had ever encountered; heck, it didn't even rank next to how weird it had been to feel the alien magic that was inside Emma. "Hey you." Sara came close to Jane. 

"What's up dear?" Jane look up from the stove. 

"Can you make enough for Emma to have some? She's having the weirdest pregnancy cravings." Sara kept her voice low. 

"Yeah, that's no trouble." Jane lowered her voice, "Is she... ok?" 

Sara nodded, "I think the food is helping her calm down, and that's helping her strength return." 

Jane breathed a sigh of relief, "Oh good, I was so scared."

Sara put an arm around Jane, "I know, me too. But I promise, I can feel her strength returning to her. Thanks to you, she's much more steady." 

Jane nodded, and let her head rest on Sara's shoulder for a moment. "I'm glad I could help. I felt so useless, then I saw Anne get food for Cass, and movie nights popped into my head. Emma always loved them, even when she was a teen."

"Movie nights?" Sara asked as she held Jane. 

"Yeah, when you... were pregnant with Anne, I used movies and popcorn to distract the twins."

"Ah, I see. Now that you mention it, I do kind of recognize the smell." Sara kissed Jane's forehead. "Good thinking dear." 

"Thank you." Jane felt her cheeks get warm. "I'll make sure there's plenty of this for Emma to have as much as she wants. It'll take a bit to cook, but I'll say something when it's ready." Jane's smile was big, it felt good knowing there was something she could do to help. 

Sara gave a little squeeze, then grabbed another bowl of popcorn for Emma. She browsed through the movie options, and had one picked out when the food was ready. 

"Dinner's ready. Want me to bring you some Emma?" Jane called out from the kitchen.

"That's ok, I think I'll come over there." Emma answered, then slowly made her way to her feet.

Sara felt tense, ready to spring to action if Emma wavered, but not wanting to hover too much. Thankfully Emma was much steadier, and made her way over to the kitchen to grab herself a plate without any issues. Sara followed, making her way to the bar instead of the food. She made a round of drinks for all three of them, while Jane went to tell Anne that Cass's dinner was ready. 

"Auntie Emma!" Cass came up and hugged Emma's legs. 

"Hey sweetie." Emma greeted. 

Cass held up her arms. "Up?"

"Not right now. Sorry." Emma did feel better than she had, but she didn't want to push her pregnant body. Not any more than she'd already had. 

"You still don't feel good?"

"I'm a little better, but I'm not all better." 

"Can you hug?" the toddler looked so sad. 

"Of course, come here." Emma pulled Cassandra close, and held her niece against her pregnant belly. She supposed in a way, she held two children. 

"What's wrong Auntie Emma? When you going to feel all better?"
