Sara took a moment to really focus on the darkness, the magic of the vampires, then focused on her senses. Sara could feel Emma was a little weak, not surprising if she wasn't getting enough blood fruit. But that wasn't the whole story; something was draining her daughter's strength. Sara sent her senses to beyond Emma's belly, and felt another magic that she hadn't felt in a while: alien magic. But it was concentrated in... Emma's womb? She shifted her senses to get a better read, and couldn't believe what she felt: it was deep in Emma's lower abdomen and small, but it was fully alien; just like the ones that had abducted Sara for a brief time. "Emma...." Sara tried to start, but she lost the words before she could say them. 

Emma waited at first, but then Sara didn't say more. Why wasn't she saying more? "What is it Mom? How bad is it?" Emma felt her fingers curl at the fear in her. What had her mother speechless? Was she going to become a ghost again? 

"No, it's not bad." It just wasn't supposed to be possible. Sara shook herself. No, clearly it was because she felt it; the dark power of the vampires was never wrong. Sara took a deep breath to center herself before trying again. "Honey, you're pregnant." 

Emma must I have heard wrong. "But I can't be..." She couldn't say the word suddenly. "I mean I haven't been with anyone, not like that." 

"The child is alien." Sara swallowed at her own disbelief, they had said vampires weren't compatible hosts. And yet, Emma somehow was one. Sara tried to think back through everything she remembered when she had been taken by aliens. 

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-35-00%20AM.png)

Emma froze and her eyes went big. "What?" She took a step back, on wobbly shaky legs. The world 'child' echoed in her head. 

That pulled Sara out of her thoughts, and helped her focus on the here and now. "Let's get you sitting down again, ok?" she helped guide Emma to her chair again, then Sara sat back down. 

Anne's face was covered in surprise, "I cannot believe we are both with child at the same time. The odds of that, it must be astronomical."

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-26-17%20AM.png)

The word 'child' kept repeating in Emma's head, until it turned into an otherworldly tone. "So that's what the grim reaper meant..." Emma put her hand to forehead. "Oh gosh." 

Emma wasn't sure if Sara or Anne said anything else, everything became a haze as she felt lightheaded and even more out of sorts than before. 

"I think I need to lay down." Emma said. She tried to stand but found herself even more unsteady on her feet. 

Sara was around the table in an instant thanks to her vampire speed. "Careful sweetie." Sara held her up. "Let me help you, ok?"

Emma bit her lip and nodded, "Ok Mom." Her voice was soft and sad. 

Sara picked up Emma, "Do you want a bed upstairs, or your coffin downstairs?"

"Maybe just the couch..." Emma whispered, she suddenly didn't want to be alone. 

Sara nodded then carried Emma over to the long couch near the fireplace. Sara lowered Emma in the middle of the couch so she could lay, then Sara sat on one end of it, and put Emma's head in her lap. She stroked her daughter's shoulder with her right hand, while she kept her right out of Emma's line of sight and squeezed it into a fist without using her vampire strength. This was a very different situation, but what had always helped Sara during her own struggles with pregnancy had been Jane's touch. The contact, and the connection. She didn't know **how** this was happening to her daughter, but really... the how didn't matter. It was happening. All she could do now was keep Emma safe as she helped her deal with it, no matter what that meant. 

At the moment, it meant staying calm and watching over her. Sara tried to keep her own breathes as even and steady as she could while she listened to Emma's heartbeat, watched Emma's breathing, and felt what she could with her vampire senses. If anything was going to happen to Emma, it wasn't going to sneak up on Sara this time. Not to mention they had death flowers; grim wouldn't take her baby girl again, Sara would make sure of that. 

Over in the kitchen, Jane just stood there. She wasn't sure what to do. Sara always knew what to do. 

Just then, Cass started to cry in the bedroom. Jane turned to the door, but she heard Anne's shaky voice before she could do anything. "I'll get her. She's probably just hungry." 

Jane turned and watched Anne get up, come into the kitchen, grab Cass's favorite snack from the fridge, and go into the bedroom in the front of the cabin where Cass had been sleeping. 

A mother comforting and feeding a daughter. Surely there was something Jane could do? She turned back to the living room, but this time her eyes landed on the TV above the fireplace. Emma always loved movie night popcorn. 

Jane wasn't sure if it would help, but she started looking through the cabinets. She was pretty sure- yes there is was. Jane put the popcorn machine on the counter. Next she put on some water to boil for tea, and let it heat up while she started looking for the ingredients for the popcorn.

Sara sat there, holding her daughter, at the moment just protecting her by being steady. She wished she could do more, but Sara knew all too well, sometimes just being a rock in the storm was already a lot.

After a few bowls of popcorn, Emma said she wanted some of the tea. So Sara helped Emma sit up and grabbed a cup for each of them. Emma took a drink, then she looked down at herself, "I guess this explains why I've felt so... tight deep inside here lately." It hadn't been there at first, but it had been slowly growing for the past few weeks. Sort of like cramps that wouldn't go away, and yet not.

"I suppose that's one way of putting it. For me, it was stretching and fullness." Sara would never forget what it had been like to carry twins back when she was human. She sipped on her own mug of tea. She wasn't thirsty, especially not for tea, but figured it might help Emma feel less alone if they both had some. 

Emma tilted her head a little, "I've had a little of that, I guess." She took a drink of her tea. 

Sara nodded; that made sense, given that Emma was a vampire instead of human. "Well, is this something you want? Being pregnant that is. You don't have to decide right away, if you want to think about it." 

Emma looked down at her midsection again. "I want a child of my own. I just had decided to wait until Cass was older."

"Life has a habit of... preferring to be unpredictable at times." So much of Sara's own life had been unexpected, from her wife Jane to her daughters to her vampirism. She hadn't expected any of it, and now she couldn't imagine her life without all of them. But that didn't mean Emma would want to continue her own unexpected pregnancy, and Sara would support whatever her daughter wanted. 

"Yes life does. I mean, I assumed I'd find somebody, and that they would be there to help me through this. Though, well, it's not like I'm alone now." Emma paused. "You're probably right Mom, that some time to think is probably a good idea, to sleep on it at least once, but I think I probably want this?"

Sara nodded again, "Take as much time as you want sweetie. I'm here for you, no matter what you choose." 

"I know, thank you." Emma smiled, then caught a whiff of what Jane was cooking. She sniffed, trying to take in more of the scent, "Oh that smells so good-" Emma stopped as her belly growled loudly. She held a hand over her painfully empty stomach, and briefly fantasized about biting and chewing what Jane was cooking. Was she *hungry* for *food*? "That's... weird." 

Sara shrugged, "Pregnant bodies do all sorts of weird things, including make odd noises." More things Sara knew from first hand experience. 

Emma wasn't sure how to explain the sensation. She was a fully transformed vampire, she couldn't actually need regular human food... right? Though, she'd felt better as she had more and more the popcorn. Better than she'd felt in weeks, which was odd. A little voice reminded her that her vampire transformation had been unusual, but it was drowned out by another whiff of the mouth water smells coming from the kitchen. She wanted whatever Jane was cooking, but that emptiness in her belly didn't want to wait. Emma turned to Sara, "I think I want some of what Jane is cooking when it's ready. And do you know if there's any popcorn left? I..." saying she was hungry didn't feel right. "I don't know, I guess just want food." 

"I'll find out dear." Sara set down her own cup of tea, then stood and headed to the kitchen. A pregnant vampire craving human food for comfort. It certainly wasn't the weirdest thing Sara had ever encountered; heck, it didn't even rank next to how weird it had been to feel the alien that was inside Emma. "Hey you." Sara came close to Jane. 

"What's up dear?" Jane look up from the stove. 

"Can you make enough for Emma to have some? She's having the weirdest pregnancy cravings." Sara kept her voice low. Well, weird for a vampire anyway. 

"Yeah, that's no trouble." Jane lowered her voice, "Is she... ok?" 

Sara nodded, "Yes, I think so. The food seems to be helping her calm down, and that's helping her strength return." 

Jane breathed a sigh of relief, "Oh good, I was so scared." They had already lost Emma once; Jane didn't know.... she just didn't know what she would do. But if Sara said yes, then their daughter would be fine. 

Sara put an arm around Jane, "I know, me too. But I promise, I can feel her strength returning to her. Thanks to you, she's much more steady." 

Jane nodded, and let her head rest on Sara's shoulder for a moment. "I'm glad I could help. I felt so useless, then I saw Anne get food for Cass, and movie nights popped into my head. Emma always loved them, even when she was a teen."

"Movie nights?" Sara asked as she held Jane. 

"Yeah, when you... were pregnant with Anne, I used movies and popcorn to distract the twins. It kinda became my go to way of keeping them away from the windows." Back when Sara had been a newly turned vampire... Sara's stomach had just withered, Sara had been pregnant with Anne, and they didn't have any bloodfruit plants yet. Even Lilith had thought feeding on people was Sara's only option. Jane hadn't wanted Emma and Savanna to see those feedings, they were only children and she didn't think they'd understand. Keeping the girls' attention on the TV had been a good way to make sure they weren't looking out the windows. 

"Ah, I see. Now that you mention it, I do kind of recognize the smell." Sara kissed Jane's forehead. "Good thinking dear." 

"Thank you." Jane felt her cheeks get warm. "I'll make sure there's plenty of this for Emma to have as much as she wants. It'll take a bit to cook, but I'll say something when it's ready." Jane's smile was big, it felt good knowing there was something she could do to help. 

Sara gave a little squeeze, then grabbed another bowl of popcorn for Emma. They browsed through the movie options as Emma munched on more popcorn, and had one picked out when the food was ready. 

"Dinner's ready. Want me to bring you some Emma?" Jane called out from the kitchen.

"That's ok, I think I'll come over there." Emma answered, then slowly made her way to her feet.

Sara felt tense, ready to spring to action if Emma wavered, but didn't want to hover too much. Thankfully Emma was much steadier, and made her way over to the kitchen to grab herself a plate without any issues. Sara followed, making her way to the bar instead of the food. She made a round of drinks for all three of them, while Jane went to tell Anne that Cass's dinner was ready. 

Jane came back, with Cass holding her hand until Cass spotted Emma. 

"Auntie Emma!" Cass came over to where Emma sat, next to her chair, as fast as her little legs would carry her. 

"Hey sweetie." Emma greeted the toddler, then looked up to Jane, "Where's Anne?"

"Feeding for two." Jane said, then made her way to the kitchen. 

Sara's smile was nostalgic, as she brought the bloodfruit drinks to the table. 

Next to Emma, Cass held up her arms. "Up?"

"Um, I'm sorry, I'm not all better yet." She did feel a little better, but not 100%, and she didn't want to push herself. 

"But what about hugs?" The toddler looked so sad. 

Emma wished she could, but that tightness in her lower abdomen had made bending or twisting difficult. 

Sara put a hand on Emma's shoulder, "Do you want me to put Cass in your lap?"

"Sure, we can try that."

Sara lifted Cass up into Emma's lap, and Emma hugged her niece against her midsection. Emma supposed, technically her pregnant belly. She didn't look pregnant, just a little bloated, but she certainly felt different physically. And now that she knew why there was that full pressure in her lower abdomen, her emotions had shifted too. It occurred to Emma that, in a way, she held two children. It made her heart feel warm. 

"What's wrong Auntie Emma? When you going to feel all better so we can play?" Cass looked up at her. 

Emma hesitated. "I'm not sure yet sweetie." Part of her wanted to say she was pregnant, but she still wanted to sleep on it. This wasn't a small decision, though part of her did wonder what it would be like to watch Cass and the alien in her play. Would they get along? 

"Get well soon kiss." Cass kissed Emma's belly through her shirt, and put her little arms around Emma middle. "Your belly feels like mommie's belly. All not squishy."

"Alright," Sara held her arms near Cass, "come on sweetie, time for food." 

Sara got Cass in the highchair, and Jane brought over a small plate of food for the toddler. "Eat up." 

Emma scooted in her chair so she could take a bite of her own plate of food. Sara sat at the table with just her drink, and Jane had a bloodfruit salad and drink. But as Emma tasted that bite of food, it was like everything else drifted away. The flavor was incredible as Emma chewed that first bite. "Mhm, oh, that so good. Thank you mom." Emma said to Jane. Part of her wanted to rush, but with how upset her gut had been lately she forced herself to go slow. Normal bites, and making sure to chew them. 

It didn't take long for Sara to finish her drink. She waited until Emma was on her second plate, then made another round for the three adults. Jane had basically finished her first glass, but Emma didn't seem to have touched her first one. Sara didn't comment, instead she simply put down the two glasses, and then sat down with her own fresh glass as she reached out with her senses. Not enough to let her dark form out, but as much as she could without doing so. What she felt was odd. Sara could feel that even more of Emma's strength had returned, more than mere comfort could account for. Her daughter had felt physically weak, even before the emotional bombshell, but even that weakness was fading from her. Sara tried to focus as much as she could without being obvious, and if anything Emma felt like a feeding vampire. Despite how her bloody mary drinks sat untouched. It was rather confusing, but Sara couldn't tell me without more deeply sensing Emma.

Emma finished her second plate, and yet some of that hungry feeling lingered. She eyed one of the glasses of liquid blood fruit. Her stomach felt better than it had in weeks, so part of her didn't even want to bother with it. Emma sighed. Sara was probably right that it would help her, she was a vampire after all. And yet, she still felt hungry for food.

Depending on rather they're carrying a vampire or a human."
