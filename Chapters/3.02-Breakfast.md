"No, it's not bad." It just wasn't supposed to be possible. Sara shook herself. No, clearly it was because she felt it. Sara took a deep breath to center herself before trying again. "Honey, you're pregnant." 

Emma must I have heard wrong. "But I can't be..." She couldn't say the word suddenly. "I mean I haven't been with anyone, not like that." 

"The child is alien." Sara swallowed at her own disbelief, they had said vampires weren't compatible hosts.

Emma froze and her eyes went big. "What?" She took a step back, on wobbly shaky legs. The world 'child' echoed in her head. 

That pulled Sara out of her thoughts, and helped her focus on the here and now. "Let's get you sitting down again, ok?" she helped guide Emma to her chair again, then Sara sat back down. 

Anne's face was covered in surprise, "I cannot believe we are both with child at the same time. The odds of that, it must be astronomical."

![](../assets/img/03.02/06-20-18_12-26-17%20AM.png)

The word 'child' kept repeating in Emma's head, until it turned into an otherworldly tone. "So that's what the grim reaper meant..." Emma put her hand to forehead. "Oh gosh." 

Emma wasn't sure if Sara or Anne said anything else, everything became a haze as she felt lightheaded and even more out of sorts than before. 

"I think I need to lay down." Emma said. She tried to stand but found herself even more unsteady on her feet. 

Sara was around the table in an instant. "Careful sweetie." Sara held her up. "Let me help you, ok?"

Emma bit her lip and nodded, "Ok Mom." Her voice was soft and sad. 

Sara picked up Emma, "Do you want a bed upstairs, or your coffin downstairs?"

"Maybe just the couch..." Emma whispered, she suddenly didn't want to be alone. 

Sara nodded then carried Emma over to the long couch near the fireplace. Sara sat on one end of it, and put Emma's head in her lap. She stroked her daughter's shoulder. This was a very different situation, but what had always helped Sara during her own struggles with pregnancy had been Jane's touch. The contact, and the connection. She didn't know **how** this was happening to her daughter, but really... the how didn't matter. It was happening. And all she could do now was help her deal with it, regardless of what that meant.

After a few bowls of popcorn, Emma said she wanted some of the tea. So Sara helped Emma sit up and grabbed a cup for each of them. Emma took a drink, then she looked down at herself, "I guess this explains why I've felt so... tight deep inside." 

"I suppose that's one way of putting it. For me, it was stretching and fullness." Sara would never forget what it had been like to carry twins. She sipped on her own mug of tea. She wasn't thirsty, especially not for tea, but figured it might help Emma feel less alone if they both had some. 

Emma nodded, "I've had a little of that I guess." She took a drink of her tea. 

Sara nodded; that made sense, given that Emma was a vampire instead of human. "Well, is this something you want? Being pregnant that is. You don't have to decide right away, if you want to think about it." 

Emma looked down at her midsection again. "I want a child of my own. I just had decided to wait until Cass was older."

"Life has a habit of... preferring to be unpredictable at times." So much of Sara's own life had been unexpected, from her daughters to her vampirism to her wife Jane. She hadn't expected any of it, and now she couldn't imagine her life without all of them. But that didn't mean Emma would want to continue her own unexpected pregnancy, and Sara would support what Emma wanted. 

"Yes life does. I mean, I assumed I'd find somebody, and that they would be there to help me through this. Though, well, it's not like I'm alone now." Emma paused. "You're probably right Mom, that some time to think is probably a good idea, to sleep on it at least once, but I think I probably want this."

Sara nodded, "Take as much time as you want sweetie. I'm here for you, no matter what you choose." 

"I know, thank you." Emma smiled, then caught a whiff of what Jane was cooking. She sniffed, trying to take in more of the scent, "Oh that smells so good-" Emma stopped as her stomach growled loudly. She held a hand over her painfully empty belly, and briefly fantasized about biting and chewing what Jane was cooking. Was she *hungry* for *food*? "That's... weird." 

Sara shrugged, "Pregnant bodies do all sorts of weird things, including make odd noises." Especially during Sara's first pregnancy, nothing had felt normal pretty much the whole time. 

Emma wasn't sure how to explain the sensation. She was a fully transformed vampire, she couldn't actually need regular human food... right? Though, she'd felt better as she had more and more the popcorn. Better than she'd felt in weeks, which was odd. A little voice reminded her that her vampire transformation had been unusual, but it was drowned out by another whiff of the mouth water smells coming from the kitchen. She wanted whatever Jane was cooking, but that emptiness in her belly didn't want to wait. Emma turned to Sara, "I think I'm craving some of what Jane is cooking. And do you know if there's any popcorn left?"

"I'll find out dear." Sara set down her cup of tea, then stood and headed to the kitchen. A pregnant vampire craving human food for comfort. It certainly wasn't the weirdest thing Sara had ever encountered; heck, it didn't even rank next to how weird it had been to feel the alien magic that was inside Emma. "Hey you." Sara came close to Jane. 

"What's up dear?" Jane look up from the stove. 

"Can you make enough for Emma to have some? She's having the weirdest pregnancy cravings." Sara kept her voice low. Well, weird for a vampire anyway. 

"Yeah, that's no trouble." Jane lowered her voice, "Is she... ok?" 

Sara nodded, "I think the food is helping her calm down, and that's helping her strength return." 

Jane breathed a sigh of relief, "Oh good, I was so scared."

Sara put an arm around Jane, "I know, me too. But I promise, I can feel her strength returning to her. Thanks to you, she's much more steady." 

Jane nodded, and let her head rest on Sara's shoulder for a moment. "I'm glad I could help. I felt so useless, then I saw Anne get food for Cass, and movie nights popped into my head. Emma always loved them, even when she was a teen."

"Movie nights?" Sara asked as she held Jane. 

"Yeah, when you... were pregnant with Anne, I used movies and popcorn to distract the twins. It kinda became my go to way of keeping them away from the windows." Back when Sara had been a newly turned vampire, they hadn't had bloodfruit yet. Which had meant feeding on people had been Sara's only option. Jane hadn't wanted Emma and Savanna to see those feedings, they were only children and she didn't think they'd understand, and keeping the girls' attention on the TV had been a good way to make sure they weren't looking out the windows. 

"Ah, I see. Now that you mention it, I do kind of recognize the smell." Sara kissed Jane's forehead. "Good thinking dear." 

"Thank you." Jane felt her cheeks get warm. "I'll make sure there's plenty of this for Emma to have as much as she wants. It'll take a bit to cook, but I'll say something when it's ready." Jane's smile was big, it felt good knowing there was something she could do to help. 

Sara gave a little squeeze, then grabbed another bowl of popcorn for Emma. They browsed through the movie options as Emma munched on more popcorn, and had one picked out when the food was ready.

"That's ok, I think I'll come over there." Emma answered, then slowly made her way to her feet.

Sara felt tense, ready to spring to action if Emma wavered, but didn't want to hover too much. Thankfully Emma was much steadier, and made her way over to the kitchen to grab herself a plate without any issues. Sara followed, making her way to the bar instead of the food. But as Emma tasted that bite of food, it was like everything else drifted away. The flavor was incredible as Emma chewed that first bite. "Mhm, oh, that so good. Thank you mom." Emma said to Jane. Part of her wanted to rush, but with how upset her gut had been lately she forced herself to go slow. Normal bites, and making sure to chew them. 

It didn't take long for Sara to finish her drink. She waited until Emma was on her second plate, then made another round for the three adults. Jane had basically finished her first glass, but Emma didn't seem to have touched her first one. Sara didn't comment, instead she sat down with her own fresh glass and reached out with her senses. Not enough to let her dark form out, but as much as she could without doing so. What she felt was odd. Sara could feel that even more of Emma's strength had returned, more than mere comfort could account for. Her daughter had felt physically weak even before Sara had given her the news about the alien pregnancy, but even that weakness was fading from her. Sara tried to focus as much as she could without being obvious, and if anything Emma felt like a feeding vampire. Despite how her bloodfruit sat untouched.
