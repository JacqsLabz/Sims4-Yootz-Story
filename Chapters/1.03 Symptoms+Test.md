After Sara and Jane are married, Sara notices some fatigue during a workout one day. The concerning part was that she just can't push through it. It concerns her, but she decided to give it a day or two to see if it was nothing and went away on it's own. 

Except a day or two later it turns into more of a wall of fatigue, and her joints don't feel right. They feel lose and unstable, a massive red flag for anyone that did the type of things she did. So when she got home she went to the bathroom to go through her normal routine of various tests to check various biomarkers, hormones, and such to see if this is something she could troubleshoot on her own or if she needs to see her doctor. As each one was ready, she started going over each test and tracking each result. Next was the pregnancy test; Sara glanced over at it. 

And it was... positive? She stopped. Then she shook herself and threw it in the trash. Stupid thing had to be defective. So she grabbed another one without recording the first one. 

Except that one came up positive too. Sara froze. It was supposed to be negative. Her hands started to shake. No, she wouldn't let herself hope. Crushed hope hurt too much. She clinched her hands into fits to steady them. She alternated between forcing her hands flat and squeezing them into fists, slow careful, but not too tights, since all her joints were messed up. It had to be a false positive. Which meant she needed a doctor's appointment so they could try to figure out why. 

All her other home tests were normal, which also meant she needed to see the doctor for why her joints weren't stable. Hopefully they could fix especially the joint issue soon. She closed her eyes, focused on her breath, and thought about the smell of the leather of her punch bag. And how here soon she would let loose on it. And how good that felt each time when she did. 

Sara threw away all the tests, left the bathroom, and used her phone to call her doctor's office. They could see her tomorrow morning. She had tomorrow off work, so that worked for her. 

Appointment scheduled, she tried to sit on the couch but her leg wouldn't stop shaking and bouncing. She found herself looking around everywhere. She saw one of Jane's paintings, an abstract one, and for a moment pictured Jane gushing about the symbolism behind why the blue water wasn't supposed to be water, the green part was. Water. Swimming was the safest exercise for bad joints, everyone knew that. Sara got up, got her swim gear, and headed to the pool. Even with the fatigue, if she dialed it back just right, she could sustain swimming for a while. Let the time pass. 

It felt weird to burn off the energy slowly, but at least she was moving while she was in the pool. It didn't work as well as her normal high intensity workouts, but it didn't risk serious joint damage and it helped take the edge off.
