Sara was in the bathroom running through her usual list of home tests. Checking the levels of multiple hormones and such in her, urine, and saliva. When they were ready she started going over and tracking each result.

And the pregnancy test was... positive? Sara stopped. Then she shook herself and threw it in the trash. Stupid thing was defective, so she grabbed another one. 

Except it came up positive too. Sara froze. It was supposed to be negative. Her hands started to shake. No, she wouldn't let herself hope. Crushed hope hurt too much. She clinched her hands into fits to steady them. It was a false positive, and everything else was normal. She alternated between forcing her hands flat and squeezing them into fists, slow careful, but not to tights, since all her joints were messed up. That meant she needed a doctor's appointment to figure out what was wrong with her joints and why they weren't stable. Hopefully they could fix it soon. She closed her eyes, focused on her breath, and thought about the smell of the leather of her punch bag. And how here soon she would let loose on it. And how good that felt each time when she did. 

Sara threw away all the tests, left the bathroom, and used her phone to call her doctor's office. They could see her tomorrow morning. She had tomorrow off work. 

She tried to sit but her leg wouldn't stop shaking. She found herself looking around everywhere. She saw one of Jane's paintings, an abstract one, and remembered when she had said the blue was the water. Water. Swimming was the safest exercise for bad joints, everyone knew that. Sara got up, got her swim gear, and headed to the pool. Even with the fatigue, if she dialed it back just right, she could sustain that for a while. 

It felt weird to burn off the energy slowly, but at least she was moving while she was in the pool. It didn't work as well as her normal high intensity workouts, but it didn't risk joint damage and it helped take the edge off.
