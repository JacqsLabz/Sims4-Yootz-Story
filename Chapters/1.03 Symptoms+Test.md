After Sara and Jane are married, Sara notices some fatigue during a workout one day. The odd part is that she just can't push through it. It concerns her, but she decided to give it a day or two to see if it was nothing and went away on it's own. 

Except a day or two later it turns into more of a wall of fatigue, and her joints don't feel right. They feel lose and unstable, a massive red flag for anyone that did the type of things she did. So when she got home she went to the bathroom to go through her normal routine of various tests to check various biomarkers, hormones, and such to see if this is something she can troubleshoot on her own or if she needs to see her doctor. As each one was ready, she started going over each test and tracking each result. She was going down her list, next was the pregnancy test, which was only one the list at her doctor's recommendation when her cycle started being irregular. In her opinion it was kind of wasteful, given everything. Sara glanced over at it. 

And the pregnancy test was... positive? She stopped. Then she shook herself and threw it in the trash. It had to be defective, she knew that was the most likely reason. So she grabbed another one without recording the first one. 

Except that one came up positive too. Sara froze. It was supposed to be negative. Her hands started to shake. No, she wouldn't let herself hope. Crushed hope hurt too much. She clinched her hands into fits to steady them. She alternated between forcing her hands flat and squeezing them into fists, slow careful, but not too tights, since all her joints were messed up. She knew the next most likely thing was that the test was a false positive. And getting a false positive on a pregnancy test meant she needed a doctor's appointment so they could try to figure out why. That was why her doctor had recommended she include it, because of how many other things could do that, and none of them were good. 

All her other home tests were normal, which also meant she needed to see the doctor for why her joints weren't stable. Hopefully they could fix especially the joint issue soon. She closed her eyes, focused on her breath, and thought about the smell of the leather of her punch bag. And how here soon she would let loose on it. And how good that felt each time when she did. 

Sara threw away all the tests, left the bathroom, and used her phone to call her doctor's office. They could see her tomorrow morning. She had tomorrow off work, so that worked for her. 

Appointment scheduled, she tried to sit on the couch but her leg wouldn't stop shaking and bouncing. She found herself looking around everywhere. She saw one of Jane's paintings, an abstract one, and for a moment pictured Jane gushing about the symbolism behind why the blue water wasn't supposed to be water, the green part was. Water. Swimming was the safest exercise for bad joints, everyone knew that. Sara got up, got her swim gear, and headed to the pool. Even with the fatigue, if she dialed it back just right, she could sustain swimming for a while. 

It felt weird to burn off the energy slowly, but at least she was moving while she was in the pool. It didn't work as well as her normal high intensity workouts, but it didn't risk joint damage and it helped take the edge off.

Actually maybe she takes 1 preg test and it just like ok that's one's broken, so she does a second one. But it's positive too. And even if she doesn't think it, she doesn't want to take a third one because she does want 3 positive ones. Metaphorically staring down two barrels is enough. 

She's not "pacing herself" she's making sure she "can do sustained movement" because of the fatigue. Because she just wants to move for the next few hours to make them disappear. She wants her mind to go elsewhere she wants to think about something else. She wants to get tired and go to sleep. She wants something that takes a lot of time. 

Also Sara doesn't believe the home pregnancy test because she knows they can be unreliable. A home urine pregnancy test is not equal to a clinic blood pregnancy test. A home urine test CAN be a false positive, but a clinic's blood test won't be a false positive. Detecting the hormone in the urine enough to make the test read positive does not always mean pregnancy is happening. A doctor measuring it in the blood doesn't get a simple yes/no, they get an amount of the hormone. It's much more complex and precise. Also measuring blood is not the same as measuring urine. Sara assumes the home test is a false positive because she knows that can happen and according to her mental model that is the next most likely thing. First is that the test is defective and the hormone isn't in her urine. Next is the test is a false positive meaning there is a reason other than pregnancy for the hormone to be in the blood. Because she sees her chances of being pregnant as that low, to her it's a percentage that's a number that's lower than the chance of a defective test and lower than the chance of a false positive. Sara was told she would probably never get pregnant, and then she started her training to be a pro athlete, which made her cycles irregular, which made her chances of getting pregnant even lower. This isn't denial, this is blunt honesty about her odds of getting what she wants. She's settled for maintaining her sanity. The Jane entered her life, so she appreciates having a partner to protect, and considers herself lucky to even have Jane in her life.
