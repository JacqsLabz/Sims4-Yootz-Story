After Sara and Jane are married, Sara notices some fatigue during a workout one day. The odd part is that she just can't push through it. It concerns her, but she decided to give it a day or two to see if it was nothing and went away on it's own. 

Except a day or two later it turns into more of a wall of fatigue, and her joints don't feel right. They feel lose and unstable, a massive red flag for anyone that did the type of things she did. So when she got home she went to the bathroom to go through her normal routine of various tests to check various biomarkers, hormones, and such to see if this is something she can troubleshoot on her own or if she needs to see her doctor. As each one was ready, she started going over each test and tracking each result. She was going down her list, next was the pregnancy test, which was only part of this at her doctor's recommendation when her cycle started being irregular. In her opinion it was kind of wasteful, given everything. Sara glanced over at it. 

And the pregnancy test was... positive? She stopped. Then she shook herself and threw it in the trash. Stupid thing had to be defective, she knew that was the most likely reason. So she grabbed another one without recording the first one. 

Except that one came up positive too. Sara froze. It was supposed to be negative. Her hands started to shake. No, she wouldn't let herself hope. Crushed hope hurt too much. She clinched her hands into fits to steady them. She alternated between forcing her hands flat and squeezing them into fists, slow careful, but not too tights, since all her joints were messed up. She knew the next most likely thing was that the test was a false positive. And getting a false positive on a pregnancy test meant she needed a doctor's appointment so they could try to figure out why. That was why her doctor had recommended she include it, because of how many other things could make it do that, and none of them were good. 

All her other home tests were normal, which also meant she needed to see the doctor for why her joints weren't stable. Hopefully they could fix especially the joint issue soon. She closed her eyes, focused on her breath, and thought about the smell of the leather of her punch bag. And how here soon she would let loose on it. And how good that felt each time when she did. 

Sara threw away all the tests, left the bathroom, and used her phone to call her doctor's office. They could see her tomorrow morning. She had tomorrow off work, so that worked for her. 

Appointment scheduled, she tried to sit on the couch but her leg wouldn't stop shaking and bouncing. She found herself looking around everywhere. She saw one of Jane's paintings, an abstract one, and for a moment pictured Jane gushing about the symbolism behind why the blue water wasn't supposed to be water, the green part was. Water. Swimming was the safest exercise for bad joints, everyone knew that. Sara got up, got her swim gear, and headed to the pool. Even with the fatigue, if she dialed it back just right, she could sustain swimming for a while. Let the time pass. 

It felt weird to burn off the energy slowly, but at least she was moving while she was in the pool. It didn't work as well as her normal high intensity workouts, but it didn't risk serious joint damage and it helped take the edge off.

## Author's Notes

Sara starts to feel tired and like her joints are lose. 

She checks a few things and takes a few tests at home. One of her standard tests is a pregnancy one, because her cycles are irregular from her intense training. 

Except instead of the routine negative result, the pregnant test comes up positive. She didn't want to let herself hope. Hop was powerful, and painful when it got crushed. No way she was pregnant Sara told herself. She blinked and swallowed, and used her phone to make an appointment for a blood test. That would come up negative, and she could start looking for the real reason for her fatigue and loose joints. 

This scene shoes a "no we do not hope for being pregnant because we gave up on that" mindset of the character Sara. This character wants her own biological children more than anything else, but she doesn't let herself hope that she'll actually have one. 

This is something that the character Sara has wanted more than anything for a long time she just like I said gave up on it because she didn't want to be repeatedly crushed by having her dreams crashed so she just didn't dream, not about pregnancy and having children and all that kind of thing. Well maybe I shouldn't say she wants to be pregnant, she doesn't actually want to be pregnant but she does want her own kid. She wants the end result of pregnancy. That doesn't mean she wants the actual process, but she is willing to go through the process to get the end result, which is a very hard thing but worth it. She's a very goal-oriented person.
